A **powerful Telegram automation bot** that:  
- Downloads **media** (images, videos, docs) from a **source channel**  
- Copies captions, cleans text, and **shortens all URLs** automatically  
- Uploads content to a **target channel** with updated captions  
- **Deletes files from your device/server** to save space  
- Works for **both private and public channels**  

All in one lightweight, customizable Python project! 🚀  

---

## ✨ Features

✅ **Full Media Handling** – Downloads and re-uploads photos, videos, documents, or audio with original quality.  
✅ **Smart Caption & URL Shortener** – Detects links and shortens them via your preferred API.  
✅ **Automatic Cleanup** – Removes files after posting to save space.  
✅ **Private & Public Channel Support** – Works seamlessly with both.  
✅ **Customizable Rules** – Filter by keywords, media type, or hashtags.  
✅ **Fast & Reliable** – Designed for efficiency and scalability.
